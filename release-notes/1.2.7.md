# TopNV 1.2.7

## Known issue on white and very light wallpapers

TopNV still does not work reliably on white or very light wallpapers. When the black band expands from the notch, parts of the menu bar can briefly turn gray. This can happen when leaving Mission Control or Show Desktop and remains unresolved in 1.2.7.

## Fixes and improvements

- The black band follows the measured height of the macOS menu bar, keeping its lower edge aligned with the menu and the rounded corners.
- The band expands more evenly when the menu returns. On secondary desktops, TopNV can detect some Mission Control exits earlier and start the animation sooner.
- Fixed a light line that could appear inside the black band when a normal app window touched the top of the screen.
- When **Show menu bar background** is enabled in macOS, TopNV leaves the native menu background visible and keeps the rounded corners. Turning the setting off restores the black band without restarting TopNV.

Requires macOS 14 or later.

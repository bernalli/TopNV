# TopNV 1.2.2

- The black band now hides in Mission Control, instead of staying visible under the Spaces bar.
- TopNV leaves the band in place when you switch Spaces, including when you go from a full-screen app back to a desktop. It no longer closes or resizes the band on a Space change.
- After your Mac wakes, or after a display change, while a full-screen app is active, the band keeps its height instead of shrinking or disappearing.
- Rounded corners are no longer removed when you switch Spaces.
- The grey menu bar notice from 1.2.1 is gone: clicking the wallpaper does not turn the menu bar black. With a very light wallpaper at the top of the screen, macOS may still show a light menu bar; this version does not change that.

Requires macOS 14 or later.

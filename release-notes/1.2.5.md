# TopNV 1.2.5

- The black menu bar stays black when you click the wallpaper, use Show Desktop or open the screenshot tool.
- The black menu bar stays black while you swipe between desktops.
- The menu bar icons are sharper on the black bar.
- In a full-screen app TopNV no longer changes the menu bar.
- Dimmed parts of some icons, and icons while you click them, can still be hard to see on the black bar.

Requires macOS 14 or later.

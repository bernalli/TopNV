# TopNV 1.2.5

- With Show Desktop, or when you click the wallpaper, the black menu bar and the rounded corners step aside, as the corners do in Mission Control. They come back when you leave the desktop.
- The black menu bar now slides out from the notch when you turn Hide Notch on, when TopNV starts and when you leave Show Desktop or Mission Control. The rounded corners appear at the end. With Reduce Motion they appear at once.
- The black menu bar stays black when you open the screenshot tool.
- The black menu bar stays black while you swipe between desktops.
- On light wallpapers the black menu bar now looks the same as on dark wallpapers: the icons, their dimmed parts and the grey circle behind an icon while you click it.
- The menu bar icons are sharper on the black bar.
- In a full-screen app TopNV no longer changes the menu bar.

Requires macOS 14 or later.
